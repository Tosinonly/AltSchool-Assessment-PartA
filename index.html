Biography

I'm Oluwatosin Adegboyega, a banking and finance graduate with four years of experience in the research industry. I've worked with research companies like TNS RMS and Hipsles to manage data, analyse trends, and produce insights for clients across different sectors. My academic background in banking and finance gave me a strong foundation in financial systems, and my research work taught me how to turn raw data into decisions.

I've also spent time around the stock exchange and stock brokerage space, which gave me a practical view of how capital markets work, how trades are executed, and how market data informs investment decisions. That exposure deepened my interest in the technology behind financial products and platforms.

I joined AltSchool's School of Engineering to build a strong technical foundation, learn how to develop software from the ground up, and combine my finance and research experience with engineering skills so I can build useful products in the financial and data space.

Why AltSchool Africa

I chose AltSchool for two reasons: to build the technical skills I've always wanted, and to earn formal recognition for them.

  • To move from analysis to engineering. My research background gave me strong analytical skills, and I wanted to pair that with real engineering ability — to build the tools and systems that power modern finance, not just analyse them.
  • For an accredited, structured program. AltSchool offers a diploma that carries real weight, and the School of Engineering will help me develop a solid foundation in software development, collaborate effectively with technical teams, and grow into a well-rounded builder.

My Goals

  1. Build strong software engineering skills. Develop a solid foundation in programming, databases, APIs, and system design.
  2. Combine finance and research with engineering. Build data-driven products and tools that solve real problems in the financial sector.
  3. Build real-world projects and grow professionally. Apply what I learn to practical work, contribute to engineering teams, and become a well-rounded engineer in the financial technology space.
